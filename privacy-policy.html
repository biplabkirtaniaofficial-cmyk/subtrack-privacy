SubTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: SepAe1bar 20, 2026

This policy explains how the SubTrack app ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is published by BusySkill Academy. Contact: biplabkirtaniaofficial@gmail.com.

1. No account, no cloud copy

SubTrack does not ask you to create an account or sign in. The subscriptions, free trials, prices, dates and notes you enter are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. We do not receive, upload or have access to this information.

2. Notifications

Reminders are created and shown on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device and usage data to deliver and measure ads. Where required by law (for example in the UK and the European Economic Area), the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings when that option is available. Learn more in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Users of the paid Lifetime Pass do not see ads.

4. Purchases

The optional Lifetime Pass is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google. We do not receive your payment card details.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, we do not run our own analytics or tracking, and we do not read your email, contacts or messages.

6. Your choices and deleting data

You can export your list from Settings, and delete everything the app stores with "Delete all my data" in Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data.

7. Children

SubTrack is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: biplabkirtaniaofficial@gmail.com.
